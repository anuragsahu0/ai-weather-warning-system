## 1. Architectural Overview

The **ERROR 404 Early-Warning Delivery & Notification Infrastructure** decouples risk detection from notification dispatch. It guarantees deterministic, auditable, and idempotent alert delivery across modular channels (`IN_APP`, `WEB_PUSH`, `EMAIL`).

```
RISK INTELLIGENCE ENGINE (Phase 8)
                 ↓
      ALERT DECISION SERVICE (Score ≥ 60 / HIGH / SEVERE)
                 ↓
         ALERT EVENT GENERATION (AI_MODEL_ASSESSMENT)
                 ↓
      USER SUBSCRIPTION MATCHING (Grid / Radius Haversine)
                 ↓
     NOTIFICATION POLICY ENGINE (Hazards, Min Level, Quiet Hours)
                 ↓
      DEDUPLICATION ENGINE (SHA-256 Hash Idempotency Key)
                 ↓
    ASYNC NOTIFICATION QUEUE (Decoupled Background Processing)
                 ↓
   WORKER & MODULAR PROVIDERS (In-App, Web Push, SMTP Email)
                 ↓
  DELIVERY RECEIPTS & DEAD-LETTER QUEUE (Exponential Backoff)
                 ↓
          AUDIT LOG REPOSITORY & OPS TELEMETRY
```

## 2. Alert vs Notification Distinction

| Concept | Definition | Multiplicity |
|---|---|---|
| **`AlertEvent`** | A meteorological risk state exceeding activation thresholds for a discrete spatial grid cell. | 1 event per elevated hazard state |
| **`NotificationRecord`** | A localized delivery attempt informing a specific subscribed user via a chosen channel. | 1 alert $\rightarrow$ $0, 1, \dots, N$ notifications |

## 4. Deduplication & Idempotency Strategy

To prevent spamming users upon frequent polling:
1. Every candidate notification computes a deterministic SHA-256 idempotency key:
   $$\text{Key} = \text{SHA256}(\text{alertId} + \text{subscriptionId} + \text{riskLevel} + \text{channel})$$
2. Repeated evaluation cycles of identical risk levels yield duplicate hits and are suppressed (`DUPLICATE_NOTIFICATION`).
3. Escalations (e.g. `HIGH` $\rightarrow$ `SEVERE`) produce a new key, triggering an authorized update notification.

---

## 5. Background Queue & Retry Policy
- **Worker Execution**: Background worker processes batches asynchronously without blocking HTTP response times.
- **Exponential Backoff**:
  $$\text{Delay}(n) = 2^n \times 1000\text{ ms}$$
- **Dead-Letter Routing**: If $\text{retryCount} \ge 3$, failed dispatches are moved to `DEAD_LETTER` with exact error diagnostics for operator inspection.

---

## 6. Location Privacy & Quiet Hours
- **Location Privacy**: Subscriptions store discrete 1.1km grid references or user-defined center points with bounding radius. No background continuous GPS tracking is performed.
- **Quiet Hours**: Configurable night schedules (e.g., 23:00 to 07:00). High and Severe events can optionally bypass quiet hours based on user preference.
