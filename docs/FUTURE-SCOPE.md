# ERROR 404 — Future Research & Engineering Scope

> **Team Brand**: `ERROR 404`  
> **Project**: AI-Driven Hyper-Local Early Warning System for Severe Weather Nowcasting  
> **Status Note**: Items below represent prospective future enhancements (**NOT CURRENTLY IMPLEMENTED** in the production base).

## 1. Prospective Enhancements (Future Work)

- **Dual-Polarization Radar Moments**: Ingestion of differential reflectivity ($Z_{DR}$), specific differential phase ($K_{DP}$), and correlation coefficient ($\rho_{HV}$) for direct hail and graupel hydrometeor classification.
- **INSAT-3D Rapid-Scan Imagery**: Direct integration of 4km thermal infrared satellite channel feeds from ISRO/IMD.
- **Edge Deployment for District EOCs**: Containerized edge inference appliances capable of local offline nowcasting in remote district emergency operation centers with intermittent satellite uplinks.
- **Hydrodynamic Urban Flood Coupling**: Integrating $1.1\text{km}$ precipitation outputs with 2D hydrodynamic surface runoff and drainage network models (e.g. SWMM).
